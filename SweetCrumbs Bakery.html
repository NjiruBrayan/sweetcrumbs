About Us

SweatCrumbs Bakery was founded in 2010 by Grace & amp; Samuel Kariuki with a single oven and a dream to bring artisan baking to Nairobi.

Over the years, we have grown into a beloved neighbourhood bakey trusted by thousands of families,offices and event planners across the city.Our bakery rise before down every single day to ensure that what lands on your table is always fresh,always flavourful and made with care.

We believe baking is an art not a factory process.That is why we never use artificial preservatives and we source our flour,eggs and dairy from certified local farms within 100km Nairobi.
